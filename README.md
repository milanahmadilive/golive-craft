# GoLive Craft

**Turn your TikTok LIVE into a Minecraft show.** Two modes, one click to go live:
- **Type to Spawn**: viewers type a mob's name in chat and it spawns next to you with their name above it. Gifts
  start events: bosses, armies, tsunamis, time rewinds and more.
- **Parkour**: gifts move you up and down a 100-level course, start a reset countdown, save you or punch you.

Made by MilanAHMADI
for the **Go Live for a Living** community. Free to use.

## ⬇ [Download GoLive Craft for Windows](https://github.com/milanahmadilive/golive-craft/releases/latest/download/GoLiveCraft-Setup.exe)

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit). About 170 MB. GoLive Craft updates itself after that.

## What you need
- A Windows PC with **8 GB of memory or more** (Minecraft and the server run on the same PC)
- **Minecraft: Java Edition**
- A TikTok account that can go LIVE, and a **TikTok sign key** from [eulerstream.com](https://www.eulerstream.com)
  (the app explains it during setup).
- Only for making your own events: a **Claude plan** (Pro or higher) and the [Claude app](https://claude.com/download)

## Install
1. Open the downloaded `GoLiveCraft-Setup.exe`.
2. If Windows says **"Windows protected your PC"**, click **More info → Run anyway**. (The installer isn't
   signed yet, so Windows doesn't know it.)
3. GoLive Craft opens and walks you through setup in about 5 minutes: your names, your TikTok sign key,
   installing everything (one button), joining in Minecraft, and trying every event on yourself.

## Go live
1. The first time: in **Modes**, press **Select mode** on Type to Spawn or Parkour, and choose its gifts in its
   **Settings** (pick any TikTok gift by its picture and name).
2. Start your TikTok LIVE.
3. In GoLive Craft, press **GO LIVE**.
4. In Minecraft: **Multiplayer → Direct Connection → `localhost`**.

Everything about a mode is in its **Settings**: its TikTok gifts, and for Type to Spawn its chat mobs, events and
world rules; for Parkour its countdown, saves, punches and more.

## Parkour
- **GoLive Parkour**: 100 themed rooms in 10 worlds, from the Meadowlands to the End, each with its own trick, on a
  sea of clouds at sunset. Every level is a bit harder than the one before, and level 100 is an arena of its own:
  four zones round the dragon egg.
- You arrive in the lobby, looking over the whole course. Its portal starts a run at level 1, and the level machine
  beside it, or the **Level Map** in your hotbar while you play, takes you back 1 level, back 10 or to any level
  (jumping ahead is a practice run). Touch the dragon egg to win, and the ender dragon flies you home.
- When a gift moves you up or down, the camera flies over the course to your new room first, so viewers see where
  you land.
- Everything happens in the game: your level and run time sit low on screen, out of your way, and the reset
  countdown shows the gift that saves you. In Parkour only gifts do something; chat doesn't spawn mobs.

## Make your own events with Claude
In GoLive Craft, open **Creator Studio**:
1. Get the [Claude app](https://claude.com/download) and sign in with your Claude plan.
2. In Claude, open the **Code** tab and choose your creations folder (Creator Studio shows it and has a
   **Copy** button).
3. Tell Claude what you want, like *"Make the Rose start a giant chicken army that explodes into cake"*. Claude
   builds it and tests it on a hidden test world.
4. Back in Creator Studio: **Try it now**, then **Keep** or **Undo**. Every change you keep is saved, and you can
   go back to any version.

## Help
In GoLive Craft, open **Help**: common fixes, and **Copy my logs for support** (your keys and passwords are
removed) to paste into your question in the community.
